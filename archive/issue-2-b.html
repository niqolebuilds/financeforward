Issue 2 — Why finance is leading AI adoption

Edisi 2 — Kenapa finance memimpin adopsi AI

Subject 44% of finance teams already deployed. The question isn't whether.

Subjek 44% tim finance sudah deployed. Pertanyaannya bukan lagi soal jadi-tidaknya.

Behind the number is a billing manager who stopped dreading the denial queue — here's the data that explains why.

Di balik angka itu ada billing manager yang berhenti takut sama antrean denial — ini data yang menjelaskan kenapa.

A billing manager told me she no longer dreads Monday's denial queue, because the first pass is already done when she logs in. Now the numbers behind that feeling.

Seorang billing manager bilang ke aku dia udah nggak takut lagi sama antrean denial hari Senin, karena penyortiran pertama udah selesai waktu dia login. Sekarang, angka-angka di balik perasaan itu.

The headline for leadership: by Q1 2026, roughly 44% of finance teams had deployed agentic AI — an increase of about 600% year over year (getaleph.com; aimagicx.com). Projected agent spend in financial services for 2026 is on the order of $50B. When nearly half your peer set has moved and the spending curve looks like that, "wait and see" quietly becomes "fall behind."

Headline buat leadership: sampai Q1 2026, sekitar 44% tim finance sudah men-deploy agentic AI — kenaikan sekitar 600% year over year (getaleph.com; aimagicx.com). Proyeksi belanja agent di financial services untuk 2026 ada di kisaran $50 miliar. Waktu hampir separuh peer group kamu udah bergerak dan kurva belanjanya kayak gitu, "tunggu dan lihat" diam-diam berubah jadi "ketinggalan."

For a hospital system, the sharpest case is in the revenue cycle. McKinsey estimates agentic automation can cut cost-to-collect by 30–60% (Figure 2.2). Concretely: a single agent can run 3,000+ claim-status checks a day — work equivalent to 5–8 full-time staff, or roughly $350K a year — while your team redirects to the exception cases that actually need a human (solventum.com; healthcarefinancenews.com). That's not trimming; that's re-basing the unit economics of collecting what we're owed.

Buat sistem rumah sakit, kasus paling tajam ada di revenue cycle. McKinsey memperkirakan otomasi agentic bisa memangkas cost-to-collect 30–60% (Gambar 2.2). Konkretnya: satu agent bisa menjalankan 3.000+ pengecekan status klaim per hari — kerja yang setara 5–8 staf full-time, atau sekitar $350 ribu per tahun — sementara tim kamu dialihkan ke kasus-kasus pengecualian yang beneran butuh manusia (solventum.com; healthcarefinancenews.com). Itu bukan sekadar pemangkasan; itu membangun ulang unit economics dari menagih apa yang jadi hak kita.

The sector is already voting with its roadmap. HFMA reports about 27% of healthcare organizations are deploying revenue-cycle AI at scale and roughly 53% are piloting (hfma.org). Add Gartner's projection — ~40% of enterprise apps embedding agents in 2026, up from under 5% in 2025 (via Deloitte) — and the capability is arriving inside the systems we already license, not as some distant bespoke build.

Sektornya udah "voting" lewat roadmap-nya sendiri. HFMA melaporkan sekitar 27% organisasi kesehatan men-deploy AI revenue-cycle secara skala penuh dan sekitar 53% masih piloting (hfma.org). Tambahkan proyeksi Gartner — ~40% aplikasi enterprise menyematkan agent di 2026, naik dari kurang dari 5% di 2025 (via Deloitte) — dan kapabilitasnya datang di dalam sistem yang udah kita lisensikan, bukan sebagai build khusus yang jauh di masa depan.

Why finance specifically? Because the work is structured, rule-bound, high-volume, and auditable — and that's precisely what agents do well and what a controls function can sign off. The governing distinction is our throughline: a chatbot answers; an agent acts. Acting shows up in cycle time, cost per task, and clean-claim rate — the metrics leadership is measured on — which is exactly why finance, not the flashier functions, is out in front.

Kenapa finance secara spesifik? Karena pekerjaannya terstruktur, berbasis aturan, volumenya tinggi, dan bisa diaudit — dan itu persis yang dilakukan agent dengan baik dan yang bisa ditandatangani fungsi kontrol. Perbedaan yang jadi acuannya adalah benang merah kita: chatbot menjawab; agent bertindak. Bertindak itu kelihatan di cycle time, cost per task, dan clean-claim rate — metrik yang jadi ukuran leadership — dan itu persis kenapa finance, bukan fungsi yang lebih flashy, ada di depan.

The controls objection has a clean answer. Every deployment keeps a human-in-the-loop gate — agent proposes, named person approves — with all actions logged under a scoped agent identity for audit. You get the throughput without giving up oversight. Net: the adoption curve, the RCM savings band, and the sector's own pilots all point the same way. This issue is your evidence pack; forward it to whoever owns the number.

Keberatan soal kontrol punya jawaban yang jelas. Tiap deployment menjaga gerbang human-in-the-loop — agent mengusulkan, satu orang yang ditunjuk menyetujui — dengan semua tindakan tercatat di bawah agent identity yang scoped buat audit. Kamu dapat throughput tanpa melepas pengawasan. Intinya: kurva adopsi, rentang penghematan RCM, dan pilot-pilot di sektor itu sendiri semuanya menunjuk ke arah yang sama. Edisi ini adalah evidence pack kamu; teruskan ke siapa pun yang pegang angkanya.
