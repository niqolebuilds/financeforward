Issue 2 — Why finance is leading AI adoption

Edisi 2 — Kenapa finance memimpin adopsi AI

Subject The quiet reason our world went first

Subjek Alasan diam-diam kenapa dunia kita duluan

A department head noticed finance was strangely comfortable with agents — and realised why our work invited them in.

Seorang kepala departemen sadar finance anehnya nyaman banget sama agent — dan mengerti kenapa pekerjaan kita mengundang mereka masuk.

A friend who runs operations at another hospital group told me something that stuck. "It's funny," he said, "we assumed our clinical teams would adopt AI first. It was finance." He sounded almost surprised. I wasn't. If you've spent a month-end in our shoes, you already know why.

Seorang teman yang menjalankan operasional di grup rumah sakit lain pernah bilang sesuatu yang nempel di kepalaku. "Lucu ya," katanya, "kita kira tim klinis bakal adopsi AI duluan. Ternyata finance." Nadanya hampir kaget. Aku nggak. Kalau kamu pernah ngalamin month-end di posisi kita, kamu udah tahu alasannya.

Think about what a day in revenue cycle actually looks like. A claim has a reason code, a payer policy, a filing deadline, a documentation trail, and a defined next action. A reconciliation has two sides that must tie. A close has a checklist that runs the same way every period. This is structured, repeatable, rule-bound, auditable work — and it turns out that's the exact terrain where an agent is most useful and easiest to trust. Remember our line: a chatbot answers; an agent acts. Finance is full of tasks that need acting on, not just answering.

Coba pikirkan seperti apa sebenarnya satu hari di revenue cycle. Satu klaim punya kode alasan, kebijakan payer, deadline pengajuan, jejak dokumentasi, dan tindakan berikutnya yang jelas. Satu rekonsiliasi punya dua sisi yang harus cocok. Satu closing punya checklist yang jalannya sama tiap periode. Ini pekerjaan yang terstruktur, berulang, berbasis aturan, dan bisa diaudit — dan ternyata itu persis medan tempat agent paling berguna dan paling gampang dipercaya. Ingat kalimat kita: chatbot menjawab; agent bertindak. Finance itu penuh dengan tugas yang butuh ditindaklanjuti, bukan cuma dijawab.

So while the headlines chased flashier uses, our profession quietly got on with it. By early 2026, roughly 44% of finance teams had deployed agentic AI — up around 600% in a year (getaleph.com; aimagicx.com). That's not a few pioneers; that's nearly half the field. Finance went first not because we're reckless, but because our work is legible enough that an agent can help and a human can check.

Jadi sementara headline mengejar penggunaan yang lebih flashy, profesi kita diam-diam sudah jalan duluan. Di awal 2026, sekitar 44% tim finance sudah men-deploy agentic AI — naik sekitar 600% dalam setahun (getaleph.com; aimagicx.com). Itu bukan cuma beberapa pionir; itu hampir separuh lapangan. Finance duluan bukan karena kita ceroboh, tapi karena pekerjaan kita cukup jelas sampai agent bisa membantu dan manusia bisa mengecek.

The part I love is that this suits how we already think. We are a controls culture. We like a trail. And agents, run properly, come with one: every action logged, a named human approving the consequential steps, scoped access under an "agent identity." Far from clashing with our instincts, the safeguards speak our language. The oversight isn't bolted on afterward — it's the reason finance could say yes.

Bagian yang aku suka adalah betapa cocoknya ini dengan cara kita sudah berpikir. Kita ini budaya kontrol. Kita suka jejak. Dan agent, kalau dijalankan dengan benar, datang dengan itu: tiap tindakan tercatat, satu manusia yang ditunjuk menyetujui langkah-langkah yang konsekuensinya besar, akses scoped di bawah "agent identity." Alih-alih bentrok dengan insting kita, safeguard-nya justru bicara dalam bahasa kita. Pengawasannya bukan ditempel belakangan — itu justru alasan kenapa finance bisa bilang iya.

None of this means the work is disappearing. It means the drudge part of it is finding a new owner, while the judgment part stays firmly with us. When my friend said "it was finance," what he really meant was: the people whose work is most structured had the most to gain and the clearest way to stay in control. That's us — and it's a good place to be standing.

Semua ini nggak berarti pekerjaannya menghilang. Artinya bagian yang berat dapat pemilik baru, sementara bagian penilaian tetap kokoh di tangan kita. Waktu temanku bilang "ternyata finance," yang dia maksud sebenarnya adalah: orang-orang yang pekerjaannya paling terstruktur punya yang paling banyak buat didapat dan cara paling jelas buat tetap pegang kendali. Itu kita — dan itu posisi yang bagus buat berdiri.

Your next step: Take two minutes and jot the three most repetitive tasks in your week. Keep the list — we'll use it in Issue 4 when we get concrete about the revenue cycle. Curious which task tops most people's lists? Reply and I'll share the tally.
Langkah berikutnya: Luangkan dua menit dan catat tiga tugas paling repetitif di minggu kamu. Simpan daftarnya — kita bakal pakai di Edisi 4 waktu kita masuk lebih konkret soal revenue cycle. Penasaran tugas apa yang paling sering muncul di daftar orang-orang? Balas dan aku bakal share hasilnya.
