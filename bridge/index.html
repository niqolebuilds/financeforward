The Bridge

This is the turn from watching to making. For fifteen issues we learned what agents are, saw them work on our own turf, named our fears, and imagined the finance function we're becoming. This edition is the bridge from that vision to reality — because a vision only becomes real when we build it. Over three issues I want to plant one mindset shift and hand you the confidence to act on it: your daily headaches are buildable ideas, you can learn this by doing, and you do not need to be an engineer. There are no wrong answers in a hackathon. Same three voices — a warm story-led A, a numbers-led B, and a complete flagship F — now in an invitational key. The destination is real: our hackathon, this December.
